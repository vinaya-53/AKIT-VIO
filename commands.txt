//Source of the Image

The image you see in rqt_image_view is a direct feed from Gazebo.

    Gazebo is the physics engine generating the "truth" of the environment.

    RViz is just a 3D visualizer for the data Gazebo publishes.

    Because you are looking at the topic /lauv/lauv/camerafront/camera_image, you are seeing what the virtual camera sensor "sees" inside the Gazebo simulation world
